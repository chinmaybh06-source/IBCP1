How the model works

The browser reads the CSV locally. It standardizes available economic indicators into directional scores, combines them into a Business Cycle Index (BCI), and separately combines labour-market indicators into a Fresher Employment Opportunity Index (FEOI). Missing columns are ignored and the score is reweighted across available data. The entry-window label is therefore a model signal, not a promise of a “perfect” job-market timing.

For serious forecasting, use consistent frequency, definitions and revisions. In particular, PLFS methodology changed from January 2025, so pre-2025 and post-2025 labour indicators should not be blindly treated as one homogeneous monthly series.
